About the firm

M/s. Vasu Gupta & Co., Chartered Accountants was established in 2024. The proprietor is CA Vasu Gupta, who qualified as a Chartered Accountant in 2023 and holds the Post Qualification Course in Information Systems Audit (ISA) from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

The firm provides services in the areas of audit and assurance, taxation, forensic review, financial advisory, regulatory compliance, and outsourced accounting. Information about services that are exclusively reserved for Chartered Accountants is shown on this website only when a visitor specifically selects it, consistent with the ICAI pull-only rule.

Sectors the firm has worked with include textiles and garments, liquor trade and retail, petroleum and fuel stations, kirana and FMCG trading, food and beverage services, real estate, educational services, and non-profit organisations.

This website is maintained as an information-only presence. Displayed particulars are limited to what ICAI permits: firm name, year of establishment, contact details, nature of services rendered, and the proprietor's name, year of qualification, other qualifications, and areas of experience.
