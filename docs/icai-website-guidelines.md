# ICAI Guidelines for Website Particulars

Source of truth for what a Chartered Accountant in practice, CA firm, or ICAI-registered Network/Alliance may publish on its website. Applies to this repo's website content (`index.html`).

Council-approved guidelines for posting particulars on websites by Chartered Accountant(s) in practice, firm(s) of Chartered Accountants in practice, and/or Network/Alliance of firms registered with ICAI.

## Website basics

- Free to create own website. No prescribed or standard format; format is flexible.
- May run on "Pull" and "Push" technology, except where restricted below.
- May mention website address on professional stationery and email.
- Website address should be as near as possible to the individual / trade / firm / Network/Alliance name. It must not solicit clients or work, or advertise attainments or services. The Ethical Standards Board (ESB) of ICAI decides in case of difficulty.
- Displayed information must not be materially variant from ICAI records.

## Pull-only rule for exclusive services

Services exclusively reserved for Chartered Accountants are pull-only:

- Show exclusive-services information only on a specific "pull" request by the visitor.
- Do not circulate exclusive-services information on own initiative, by email, or by any other mode/technique, except on specific pull request.
- Do not issue any circular, advertisement, or other material soliciting people to visit the website.

## Network / Alliance display

- May display ICAI Registration Number on the website.
- Must mention the networking model adopted: Alliance Model, Network Model, or Lead Firm Model.
- Must update any change in constitution, including entry or exit of any firm.
- Mention no entity that is not part of the Alliance/Network.

## Permitted particulars

The following may be displayed:

1. Member / Trade / Firm name / Network/Alliance name.
2. Year of establishment.
3. Address of Head Office and Branches; Tel. No(s); Fax No(s); E-mail ID(s).
4. Peer Review status.
5. Audit Quality Maturity Model (AQMM) level as reviewed by peer reviewer / AQMM Reviewer.
6. Nature of services rendered. Exclusive services displayable only on specific pull request.
7. Partners (Network/Alliance: firm-wise details):
   - Name
   - Year of Qualification
   - Other Qualification(s)
   - Tel. (Off.-Direct, Res., Mobile)
   - E-mail address
   - Area of Experience
8. Employees (Professional / Others):
   - Name
   - Designation
   - Area of Experience
9. Job vacancies for the CA / firm (including articleship).
10. Number of articled assistants.
11. Affiliation with a Network registered with the Institute.
12. Client name and nature of assignment handled, subject to client permission. For exclusively reserved services, only client name may be mentioned, subject to client permission. Fee charged must not be given, except:
    - Fee may be disclosed only where required by a regulator (whether or not constituted under statute, in India or outside India), only to the extent required, and only while the member works under that regulator / requirement.
    - Where so disclosed, include directly below the disclosure, in italics: "This disclosure is in terms of the requirement of [name of the regulator] having jurisdiction in [name of the country/area where such regulator has jurisdiction] vide [Rule/Directive etc. under which the disclosure is required by the Regulator]."

## Content permissions

- Passport-style photographs of associated persons and photo gallery of professional event(s) organized by the firm / Network/Alliance, or professional event(s) where partners / proprietor delivered lectures: permitted.
- Blogs, articles, professional information, bulletin boards, professional updation, and other matters of larger importance or professional interest: permitted. Educational videos on topics of professional relevance: permissible.
- Chat rooms amongst ICAI members, and between Firm / Network/Alliance and its clients: permitted, observing confidentiality protocol.
- Document management facility with distinct login and password for clients to access copies of their documents: permitted.
- Link to the firm's page on social networking site: permitted. Do not solicit people to visit or like the page.
- Online advice to clients who specifically request it, free or on payment: permitted.
- Link to websites of ICAI, its Regional Councils and Branches, and Government / Government Departments / Regulatory authorities / other Professional Bodies: permitted.
- Proprietor / partner (including of a constituent firm of a Network/Alliance) may mention existing / past position(s) held in ICAI as elected representative of Central Council, Regional Council, and Branches.

## Prohibitions and duties

- Design website details so they do not amount to soliciting clients or professional work.
- Content or technical feature contravening the Code of Conduct and Ethics, the Schedules to the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, or ICAI guidelines/directions attracts disciplinary action suo moto or on complaint under the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949.
- Ensure adequate secrecy of client matters handled through the website.
- No advertisement in the nature of banner or any other nature is permitted.
- Website must befit the profession and contain no unbecoming information or material.
