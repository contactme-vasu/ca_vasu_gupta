Privacy note

This is a static, information-only website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, offers no accounts, and collects no personal data on its own. There are no forms, no chat rooms, no document uploads, and no tracking of visitors.

If you contact the firm on your own initiative using the published contact particulars (mobile, email, or LinkedIn), your message is used only to respond to your query. Client matters are kept confidential, and no client name or assignment is published without the client's permission, consistent with ICAI requirements.

Machine-readable copies of this site (the sitemap and the markdown twin) contain the same public information as the visible pages and no personal data beyond the firm's published contact particulars.

External links (for example LinkedIn) are governed by those sites' own privacy practices. This note was last reviewed on 2026-09-20.
